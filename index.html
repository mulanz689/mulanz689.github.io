Selected research

A connected research trajectory

Mechanistic interpretability

Selective Rule Updating & Mechanistic Evaluation

Preregistered experiments on Qwen2.5-Instruct (1.5B and 14B) asking whether a model's handling of selectively updated rules can be traced to an identifiable internal mechanism. Residual-stream probes show that rule applicability is highly decodable from hidden states; controlled activation interventions, however, changed an internal readout without changing the model's final answers. The work therefore reports decodability and stops short of claiming a dominant causal mechanism.

mechanistic interpretability · selective rule updating · residual-stream probes · activation interventions · decodability vs. causal control

AI agents · finance

Intent Drift Detection in Financial AI Agents

Studies ambiguity and drift in financial agent workflows and develops an independent multi-engine architecture for evaluating whether agent execution remains consistent with an authorized intent specification. On the 850 author-injected traces of the IDV-1000 benchmark the architecture reaches 93.9% F1.

agent reliability · financial AI · intent drift · runtime evaluation

Agent systems

Constraint Availability and Enforceability

Investigates a systems-level failure mode in which a policy constraint may exist upstream yet fail to reach any component capable of enforcing it. In the audited stack, 33 of 88 bundled skills were truncated at a 10,000-code-point limit, and one documented trading-use restriction was lost before it reached the model. These are findings about whether a constraint was structurally available, not about how the agent subsequently behaved.

agent architecture · constraint propagation · system reliability · auditability

Research approach

I care about experiments that can be wrong.

I am especially drawn to problems where a surprising model behavior can be turned into a controlled experiment: isolate the relevant variable, construct competing explanations, and design a test that can falsify the preferred one.

Building real systems has also made me interested in failures that arise between components—not only inside a model—and in preserving the distinction between capability, information availability, and execution.
